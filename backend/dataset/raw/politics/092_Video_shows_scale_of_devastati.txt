Video shows scale of devastation in Stourbridge

Video shows scale of devastation in Stourbridge Footage sent to the BBC shows destroyed houses in Stourbridge, with scorched earth and burnt out trees nearby. The video was taken on Thursday night by someone helping in the aftermath, after multiple fires tore through the West Midlands. In an update this morning, West Midlands Fire Service said it had crews "tactically working" across several sites. Video shows scale of devastation in Stourbridge.
