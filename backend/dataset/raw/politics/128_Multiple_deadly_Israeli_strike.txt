Multiple deadly Israeli strikes in Gaza in under 24 hours Verified videos show the aftermath of Tuesday and Wednesday's strikes in Gaza City which show several injured people, including women and children. The Hamas-run health ministry said multiple Palestinians had been killed. The Israeli military said it was targeting Hamas commanders and that it had taken measures to mitigate harm to civilians prior to conducting strikes.
